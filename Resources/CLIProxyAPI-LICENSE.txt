PainelCli CLIProxyAPI engine provenance
---------------------------------------
The bundled engine is built from router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI v8.0.16 at commit
`a2976eb8a303f11b4ea5177bce9f9ff752634dfc`, with the local
`CLIProxyAPI-loopback.patch` applied by PainelCli. The patch restricts OAuth
callback listeners to verified loopback addresses and preserves the upstream
OAuth redirect URI and flow. This engine is a local adaptation and is not
represented as an OpenAI- or Anthropic-approved integration.
